Lickety Split

Flash and update the ESP32 devices

Flash over USB

Plug the device into this computer with a USB cable, then press Install. Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (not a phone or Safari). A first install erases the device; Wi-Fi can be set right after flashing.

Update over Wi-Fi

Sends the newest build to a device that's already running. Settings on the device are kept. The device restarts when it's done.
